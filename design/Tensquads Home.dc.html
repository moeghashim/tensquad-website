How it works

Set up in an afternoon, not a quarter

  1. 1 Tell us about the business New or existing, what you sell, where you sell it. A couple of sentences is enough to brief the squad.
  2. 2 Pick your agents and connect Choose the agents you want and connect your store, inbox and ads. Each agent gets its own isolated machine and only the keys it needs.
  3. 3 Set the limits, then let go Decide what agents can do alone and what needs a yes from you. Loosen the limits as you build trust.
